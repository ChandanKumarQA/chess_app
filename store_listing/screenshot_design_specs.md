# 📸 Play Store Screenshot Design Specifications

## Canvas Dimensions
* **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (Standard Android Smartphone Portrait)
* **Recommended Resolution:** 1080 × 1920 px or 1440 × 2560 px
* **Format:** PNG (24-bit, non-alpha)

---

## Color Palette & Typography
* **Primary Brand Colors:**
  * Deep Dark Navy / Wood Background: `#141414` & `#1E2632`
  * Luxury Gold Highlight: `#FFD700`
  * Vibrant Green (Moves / Check): `#4CAF50`
  * White Text: `#FFFFFF`
* **Typography:** Bold modern sans-serif (e.g. Montserrat, Inter, or Poppins Bold) for headers; Clean readable medium weight for subheaders.

---

## Detailed 8-Slide Production Guide

### Slide 1: Primary Value Proposition
* **Top Header:** `PLAY CHESS OFFLINE`
* **Sub-Header:** `No WiFi or Account Needed`
* **Visual Focus:** High-resolution wooden board with active game, sleek piece styling, and a floating badge: `✈️ Works Anywhere`.

### Slide 2: Tactical Puzzles
* **Top Header:** `1,000+ TACTICAL PUZZLES`
* **Sub-Header:** `Forks, Pins, Skewers & Checkmates`
* **Visual Focus:** Tactical puzzle screen with bright green arrows illustrating a Queen-Knight royal fork, showing "+50 XP" reward banner.

### Slide 3: Smart AI Bots
* **Top Header:** `CHALLENGE SMART AI BOTS`
* **Sub-Header:** `From Beginner (400) to Master (2000+)`
* **Visual Focus:** Adaptive bot selection carousel with distinctive bot avatars (e.g., Martin, Maya, Elena) and rating crowns.

### Slide 4: Daily Challenges & Streaks
* **Top Header:** `DAILY PUZZLES & STREAKS`
* **Sub-Header:** `Sharpen Your Mind Every Single Day`
* **Visual Focus:** Daily puzzle solved victory dialog showcasing the `🔥 7-Day Streak` icon and XP leaderboard progression.

### Slide 5: Endgame Training
* **Top Header:** `MASTER CRITICAL ENDGAMES`
* **Sub-Header:** `Lucena, Philidor & Pawn Theory`
* **Visual Focus:** Rook and King endgame diagram on clean slate board with step-by-step master technique callouts.

### Slide 6: Pro Analysis Board
* **Top Header:** `PRO CHESS ENGINE ANALYSIS`
* **Sub-Header:** `Detect Blunders & Find Best Moves`
* **Visual Focus:** Analysis screen showing the evaluation bar (+2.4 Advantage), move history notation list, and engine arrow suggestions.

### Slide 7: Pass & Play Multiplayer
* **Top Header:** `2-PLAYER PASS & PLAY`
* **Sub-Header:** `Play With Friends on One Screen`
* **Visual Focus:** Top-down symmetric board layout configured for tabletop two-player game with clean captured piece trays.

### Slide 8: Tournament Chess Clock
* **Top Header:** `DIGITAL CHESS CLOCK`
* **Sub-Header:** `Blitz, Rapid & Bullet Increments`
* **Visual Focus:** Clean digital dual chess clock displaying 05:00 vs 04:32 with sleek tap zones and tournament presets.
